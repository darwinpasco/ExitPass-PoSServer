## 1. Control rules

All scenarios are specifications only. `NOT_AUTHORIZED_FOR_EXECUTION` applies until a separate authority identifies the exact commit, environment, dataset, roles, scenario subset, and external-decision posture. Inputs must be synthetic, PHP-denominated, and bound to an isolated Site POS Server/fiscal identity scope. Expected calculations are those in the approved Accounting profile; no row invents an accounting result.
