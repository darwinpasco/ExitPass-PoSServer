# ExitPass POS Server Z-012D Annex E-1 Controlled UAT External Confirmation Tracker v1.0

## 1. Purpose and authority

This tracker carries forward every unresolved Annex E-1 external confirmation recorded by the canonical Annex E package as of Z-012D. It supports preparation only under `AUTHORIZED_FOR_Z012D_CONTROLLED_UAT_PREPARATION`. It does not answer, approve, or close any decision.

The exact approval requests and acceptable evidence are governed by [the External Confirmation Closure Package](ExitPass_POS_Server_Annex_E1_External_Confirmation_Closure_Package_v1.0.md). The status and safe posture below are grounded in [the Z-012C gate revalidation](ExitPass_POS_Server_Z012C_Annex_E1_Controlled_UAT_Gate_Revalidation_v1.0.md).

## 2. Status vocabulary

- `UNRESOLVED`: no exact governed confirmation is committed.
- `PREPARATION_ALLOWED`: documentation, synthetic scenario design, evidence-template design, and environment planning may continue.
- `DATA_ASSIGNMENT_NOT_AUTHORIZED`: no Controlled UAT dataset may be assigned under Z-012D.
- `EXECUTION_BLOCKED`: the decision must be resolved before the affected Controlled UAT scenario is executed.
- `ZERO_ONLY_EXECUTION_BLOCKED_PENDING_AUTHORIZATION`: governed zero evidence may be specified, but the decision still blocks execution until the accepted zero/blank representation is confirmed.
- `ZERO_ONLY_EXECUTION_NOT_BLOCKED_BY_DECISION`: the decision does not block a governed zero-only scenario, but Z-012D still does not authorize execution and nonzero behavior remains fail closed.
- `EXECUTION_NOT_BLOCKED_BY_DECISION`: the decision does not independently block the specified local, non-destructive scenario; it grants no execution authority.
- `EXTERNAL_DELIVERY_BLOCKED`: no workbook may be delivered outside the authorized local boundary.
- `DOES_NOT_INDEPENDENTLY_BLOCK_DELIVERY`: the decision is not itself a transport gate; external delivery remains unauthorized under the package-wide boundary.
- `PRODUCTION_BLOCKED`: Production use remains unauthorized.
